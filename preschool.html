Experience Preschool is a comprehensive, play-based curriculum designed for children ages 3–5 years. Each month, you’ll receive the content and supplies to deliver engaging, developmentally appropriate activities that build essential skills across all learning domains.

What's inside each Preschool Curriculum kit

 

Teacher Guides

Weekly guides that provide lessons and strategies to facilitate meaningful learning experiences.

Books

Two original thematic books to support early learning and engagement.

Literacy & Language Tools

Storybooks, storytelling materials, and writing resources that support vocabulary, comprehension, and early reading and writing skills.

Music & Movement

Original Songs, movement activities, and guided experiences that build rhythm, coordination, and active participation.

Math, Science & Exploration Materials

Hands-on manipulatives, games, and open-ended resources that support early math concepts, scientific thinking, creativity, and problem-solving.

Creative Play & Expression

Art materials and creative projects that support imagination, creativity, and fine motor development, with materials provided for each child.

Classroom Environment & Routines

Circle time displays, posters, and visual supports that help structure the day and support whole group learning.

Family Engagement

Newsletters and take-home resources that keep families informed and connected to classroom learning.
