Common questions

When will I be charged, and when does my kit arrive? +

You will be charged at the time of your initial purchase, and then automatically billed for each upcoming subscription order, which will be placed on the 10th of each month.

All initial orders are placed for the next month’s curriculum and will be delivered before the end of the month so you’re ready to start on day one.

For example, if you place an order on May 8th, you are ordering curriculum for June, and your order will arrive before the end of May. Subsequent subscription orders will also arrive before the end of the month.

Once your curriculum ships, you will receive an email notification containing a clickable link to view your tracking and delivery details.

Can I skip a month or cancel? +

Yes. You can skip an upcoming shipment for one month directly from your subscription portal.

You can contact our Support team to cancel an existing Experience Curriculum subscription.

All order and subscription modifications, billing changes, and shipment updates must be submitted by the 1st of the month before your desired effective date.

For example, if you want a change to apply to your November curriculum, you must submit the request by October 1st. Requests submitted after the 1st will apply to the following month’s curriculum.

I am homeschooling one child. Which option do I pick? +

Choose the curriculum option that matches your child’s age. For plans that allow you to select a classroom size, choose the smallest available option.

What’s included in my subscription? +

An Experience Curriculum subscription provides teaching materials and resources tailored to different age groups and classroom sizes. The exact contents depend on your selected plan.

Baby Curriculum

The Teacher Pack includes 36 shared experience cards, planning tools, storytelling puppets, rhyme posters, two board books, and a toy. The Child Pack is sold separately and adds a daily traveling journal, shared experience stickers, and weekly engagement tracking forms.

Toddler Curriculum

Designed for ages 18–36 months and available in sizes from Small for 1–4 children to Extra Large for 12–16 children. It includes four weekly teacher guides, circle-time materials, hands-on learning tools, and four books per month.

Preschool Curriculum

Designed for 1–25 children. Your kit includes four weekly teacher guides, weekly lesson packs with books, games and puzzles, manipulative math and literacy tools, and individually wrapped supply bags with craft materials for each child.

Multi-Age Box

This includes the standard Preschool Curriculum plus a Little Learners Teacher Guide with instructions for adapting preschool activities, games, and crafts for toddlers using the same supplies.

How can I track my shipment? +

Once your order ships, you’ll receive a confirmation email with tracking information and a link to view delivery details.
